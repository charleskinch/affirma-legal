Privacy policy

Ancra · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Data controller: Risi Karl Kanani, Bern, Switzerland. Contact: ancra.support@atomicmail.io.

Ancra is an audio affirmations app. We collect the minimum needed to run it, we sell no data and we use no advertising SDK.

What we collect

  • Account: e-mail address, or your Apple / Google identifier if you sign in with them.
  • Profile: first name (optional), voice, sound and wording preferences, wake-up and bedtime, onboarding answers.
  • What you create: playlists, typed affirmations, recordings of your own voice, the goal you write or speak, gratitude journal.
  • Usage: sessions played, duration, day streak, anonymised usage events.
  • Purchases: subscription status (trial, active, expired), handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.
  • Technical: crash reports and device information (model, iOS version).

Why

To run the app and sync your content across devices; to generate your personalised affirmations; to send the reminders you turned on; to manage your subscription; to fix bugs and improve the app.

Processors

  • Supabase — database, authentication and storage, hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Anthropic — generating affirmations from your goal. Only the text of your goal is sent.
  • PostHog (EU) — usage statistics.
  • Sentry — crash reports.
  • Apple — payments and notifications.

Some processors may handle data outside Switzerland or the EU; appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses) apply in that case.

Retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings › Delete my account), everything is erased within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP) and the GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection. Write to ancra.support@atomicmail.io. You may also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or the authority in your country.

Minors

The app is not intended for anyone under 16 without a parent's consent.

Wellbeing content

Ancra is a personal development tool. It does not replace medical or psychological advice.
